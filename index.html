Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get a price quote for my repair?

Because part costs vary by exact model, simply call us at 705-816-HELP (4357) or send a message using the form below with your device model (for example, "iPhone 14", "Samsung Tab A8", or "PlayStation 5") and what's happening. We'll give you a clear, upfront quote before any work begins.

Will I lose my photos, contacts, or game saves during a repair?

In the vast majority of hardware repairs—like screen replacements, batteries, charging ports, and console HDMI ports—all of your data stays 100% untouched. We never wipe your device without asking you first.

How long do repairs usually take?

Many common phone screen and battery repairs can be completed the same day when parts are in stock! Tablet glass adhesive curing and gaming console micro-soldering (like HDMI ports) typically take 24 to 48 hours so we can thoroughly test your device before returning it.

What if my device is beyond repair or not worth fixing?

I will always be 100% honest with you. If a repair costs more than the device is worth, I will let you know right away and can help you transfer your data onto a replacement device instead.
